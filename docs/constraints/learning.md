# Learning Constraints

## Direction

Learning is part of HRMS, not a detached LMS.

## Scope

Learning content, enrollments, progress, recommendations, and feedback must obey the same legal-entity and org-unit scope model as HR data.

## AI Use

AI may generate plans, quizzes, summaries, and recommendations.

AI must not turn learning signals into formal promotion, pay, or termination decisions.

## RAG

Published learning materials can enter RAG with explicit document scopes and citations.
