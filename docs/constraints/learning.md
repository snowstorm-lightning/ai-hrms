# Learning Constraints

## Direction

Learning and Co-Growth are the AI-HRMS growth engine layer, not a detached LMS and not the whole product.

## Scope

Learning content, enrollments, progress, recommendations, Co-Growth missions, work journals, and evidence portfolios must obey the same legal-entity and org-unit scope model as HR data.

## AI Use

AI may generate plans, quizzes, summaries, and recommendations.

AI must not turn learning signals into formal promotion, pay, hiring, termination, or performance-rating decisions. Learning signals can support coaching, reflection, evidence portfolios, and aggregate capability trends only.

## RAG

Published learning materials can enter RAG with explicit document scopes and citations.
